# EFDI

_2026-05-20:_ The Eastern Flank Deterrence Initiative, operationalized by NATO, envisions a forward zone where only unmanned systems operate with linked sensors, drones, and long-range fires detecting and targeting invading forces before humans enter the kill zone.
