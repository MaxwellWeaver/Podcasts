# US-India relations

## Running summary

_2026-06-18:_ India's External Affairs Minister Jaishankar called Secretary Rubio on June 12 to protest the killing of three Indian seafarers by American strikes in the Gulf of Oman on June 9, aboard a Palau-flagged tanker the US said violated the blockade on Iranian-linked shipping. Rubio's response offered neither apology nor acknowledgment of the deaths. The Foreign Policy assessment is that the diplomatic fracture between Washington and New Delhi will outlast the war, affecting Indo-Pacific strategy, energy markets, and American deterrence credibility.
