# Autonomous combat aircraft

## Running summary

_2026-06-18:_ The Air Force awarded production contracts to General Atomics (FQ-42A Dark Merlin) and Anduril (FQ-44A Fury) for Collaborative Combat Aircraft, moving the program four months ahead of schedule. The deliberate dual-contract approach hedges against technical setbacks while maintaining competitive pressure on autonomy software. Cost target is under thirty million dollars per unit, with the Air Force requesting 1.4 billion in development funding for fiscal 2027 and targeting more than 150 combat-capable drones by end of decade.
