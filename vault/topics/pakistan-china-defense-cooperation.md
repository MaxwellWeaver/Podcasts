# Pakistan-China defense cooperation

## Running summary

_2026-05-06:_ Pakistan commissioned the first of eight Chinese 039A Yuan-class attack submarines on April 30th in the presence of President Asif Ali Zardari, named Hangor after the submarine that sank an Indian frigate in 1971. The $5 billion 2015 deal includes technology transfer for four boats built in Pakistan, all eight scheduled for service by 2028, signaling Beijing's willingness to transfer advanced air-independent propulsion technology it only recently fielded.
