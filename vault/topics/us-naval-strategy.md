# US naval strategy

## Running summary

_2026-05-15:_ The Pentagon announced the Low-Cost Containerized Munitions program to deliver 10,000 small cruise missiles in three years, addressing the lesson across multiple theaters that expensive precision munitions exhaust faster than industrial capacity can replace them. Chief of Naval Operations Admiral Daryl Caudle called the Navy's post-Cold War abandonment of nuclear surface propulsion 'one of the largest mistakes,' backing nuclear propulsion for Trump-class battleships and citing refueling difficulties during Iran operations.
