# US-Iran war

## Running summary

_2026-06-15:_ After 100+ days of fighting, US and Iran agreed to cease military operations on all fronts including Lebanon, with the Strait of Hormuz reopening and the naval blockade to be lifted. A formal memorandum is scheduled for signing Friday in Geneva, though a 60-day negotiation period beginning after the MOU will address unresolved structural issues (Iran's nuclear program, proxy networks, frozen assets, sanctions architecture). BBC assessment: the war consumed American deterrent credibility without producing strategic outcome—the US burned through hard-to-replace weapons stocks, damaged relationships with Gulf monarchies, and ended roughly where it started.
