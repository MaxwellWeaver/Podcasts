# Drone warfare

## Running summary

_2026-05-04:_ Russia upgraded Shahed drones with turbojet engines to 460 mph and 29,000-foot altitude, rendering NATO's slower propeller-based interceptor tactics obsolete; analysis argues fast cruise-missile munitions, not drones, will win the next air war.

_2026-05-15:_ Ukraine's domestically produced FPV attack drones, maritime surface drones, and deep-strike platforms iterated faster than Western analogues under live battlefield conditions and are now becoming export products; Ukraine plans to purchase 25,000 unmanned ground vehicles by end of June. Ukrainian veterans trained Latvian troops on wheeled combat robots at NATO's first significant UGV exercise in Latvia, less than 200 kilometers from the Russian border.
