# F-35 readiness crisis

## Running summary

_2026-06-13:_ A GAO report released Thursday shows only one in four F-35s is fully mission capable, with the full mission capable rate dropping from 38% in fiscal 2021 to 25% in fiscal 2025, and the mission capable rate falling from 67% to 44%, driven by software delays, spare parts shortages, and corrosion; reaching the 80% target by 2030 is now estimated to cost 13.7 billion dollars more than previously planned.
