# NATO eastern flank deterrence

## Running summary

_2026-05-20:_ NATO operationalized the Eastern Flank Deterrence Initiative (EFDI), envisioning a forward zone where only unmanned systems operate with linked sensors, drones, and long-range fires designed to detect and target invading forces before human soldiers enter the kill zone, directly modeled on Ukraine's experience of catastrophic costs against sensor-drone networks.
