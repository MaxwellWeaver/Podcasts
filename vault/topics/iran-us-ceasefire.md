# Iran-US ceasefire

## Running summary

_2026-06-18:_ The fourteen-point US-Iran memorandum entered force on June 19 with a sixty-day ceasefire, reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, and a pathway to three hundred billion dollars in reconstruction relief. Core hard issues—nuclear program, proxy networks, frozen assets, sanctions—are deferred to the sixty-day negotiation window. Trump stated at the G7 that if no final deal is reached in sixty days, 'we go back to bombing.' Within hours of the text becoming public, the first commercial tankers crossed the strait, and Israel immediately expanded its occupation zone in Lebanon, testing the accord's regional architecture before the formal signing ceremony.
