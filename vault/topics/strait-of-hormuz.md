# Strait of Hormuz

## Running summary

Major war-risk insurers terminated coverage within 48 hours; shipping companies stopped transiting. Strait remains effectively closed for over two months. War on the Rocks analysis notes Russia could apply same playbook in Baltic or Black Seas.

_2026-05-14:_ Traffic collapsed 80% from 56 to 7 tankers per day since late February through drone strikes and insurance withdrawal. Iran now allows Chinese tankers through while blocking Western-linked shipments, creating two-tier system as instrument of Chinese alignment.

_2026-05-15:_ Traffic collapsed 80% since late February—from 56 tankers per day to 7—through insurance withdrawal and Iranian drone/mine threats, with Iran allowing Chinese-linked tankers while blocking Western shipping. A UK and France-led coalition of potentially 40 nations is forming to reopen the strait once a ceasefire is declared, spanning destroyers to drones, marking the most concrete European-led counter-blockade initiative and suggesting U.S. operational bandwidth is stretched.

_2026-05-21:_ Iran established the Persian Gulf Strait Authority claiming armed forces oversight over 22,000+ square kilometers extending into Omani and UAE waters, with IRGC media demonstrating punishment strikes on tankers.
