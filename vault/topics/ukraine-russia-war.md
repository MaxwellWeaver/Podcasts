# Ukraine-Russia war

## Running summary

_2026-05-04:_ Ukrainian drones struck Moscow for three consecutive nights including a residential building 10km from the Kremlin while Russia claims 100+ drone interceptions; Kyiv's Patriot interceptor supply now competes with U.S. Gulf priorities.

_2026-05-06:_ Ukraine's unilateral ceasefire lasted less than 24 hours; Russia attacked a kindergarten in Sumy Wednesday morning killing one woman and struck Zaporizhzhia Tuesday killing twelve civilians. Zelensky said Russia 'spurned the ceasefire' and Ukraine would 'act in kind.' Ukrainian drones had struck Moscow for three consecutive nights prior, including hits near a residential building ten kilometers from the Kremlin.

_2026-05-07:_ This is the third consecutive ceasefire cycle to collapse. IMF upgraded Russia's 2026 growth forecast from 0.8% to 1.1%, while Ukraine's was downgraded from 4.5% to 2%. A War on the Rocks reassessment upgraded outlook from dire to 'not great, but better than expected,' citing Russia's structural asset of sheer size and fossil fuel revenue.

_2026-05-08:_ Fourth ceasefire cycle collapsed following established pattern. Victory Day parade will contain no military hardware for first time in nearly two decades due to drone threat; foreign leader attendance reduced to Belarus, Malaysia, Laos. Russia threatened massive missile strike on Kyiv if parade is attacked. IMF upgraded Russia's 2026 growth forecast while cutting Ukraine's.

_2026-05-15:_ Ukraine secured its first legal channel to sell weapons to the U.S. through a State Department memorandum routing drone technology into joint ventures on American soil, signed 4 bilateral export contracts this week and pursuing roughly 20 more. At a NATO eastern-flank summit in Bucharest on May 13, Zelensky framed this as 'bilateral Drone Deals' using Europe's production capabilities and Ukrainian combat-validated expertise.
