# Russia sanctions enforcement

## Running summary

_2026-06-15:_ Britain conducted its first-ever boarding of a Russian shadow fleet vessel—the crude oil tanker Smyrtos in the English Channel—using Royal Marines, National Crime Agency personnel, Chinook and Merlin helicopters, P-8A patrol aircraft, and HMS Sutherland, coordinated with French counterparts. This is the first physical enforcement action against the shadow tanker network in British waters.
