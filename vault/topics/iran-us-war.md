# Iran-US war

## Running summary

_2026-05-06:_ Oil prices fell $11 a barrel after reports of a one-page US-Iran ceasefire framework under formal review in Tehran, the first concrete signal of a diplomatic off-ramp. However, Iran continues striking UAE targets and threatening Strait of Hormuz shipping with 'decisive action' against deviating vessels, while Joint Chiefs Chairman Dan Caine confirmed Iran attacked US forces ten times during the ceasefire period.
