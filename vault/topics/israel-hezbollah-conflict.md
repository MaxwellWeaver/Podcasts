# Israel-Hezbollah conflict

## Running summary

_2026-05-26:_ Netanyahu ordered intensification of strikes; Israel conducted over 100 strikes against Hezbollah infrastructure overnight Sunday-Monday, one of the heaviest bombardments since the mid-April ceasefire, killing 11 in Bekaa Valley's Mashghara village. Hezbollah deployed fiber-optic guided drones immune to Israeli jamming, forcing Israel to pursue kinetic rather than electronic solutions.

_2026-05-27:_ Israel ordered evacuation of Tyre as military expands ground operations north of the Litani River; more than one hundred strikes hit Hezbollah infrastructure in the Bekaa Valley, representing qualitative escalation beyond the mid-April ceasefire framework.

_2026-05-28:_ Israel struck Beirut for the second time since the mid-April ceasefire, targeting Ali al-Husni of the Imam Hossein Division, while expanding ground operations north of the Litani River and conducting over one hundred strikes on Hezbollah infrastructure in the Bekaa Valley; Hezbollah is deploying fiber-optic guided drones that defeat Israeli electronic jamming.
